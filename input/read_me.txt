Marvel Crisis Protocol August 2026 Character updates

This zip file contains extracted images from the character card update released by Atomic Mass Games on August 10, 2026. 

The card for Mephisto is not included because his superpower names were not present on the card. Please find the original documents at https://www.atomicmassgames.com/mcp-rules/

Also note, several of the cards released included white outlines around graphic design elements. These will appear when the cads are printed and could not be removed from the PDF as published.
